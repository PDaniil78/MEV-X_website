Four systems, four mechanisms

Each product captures value at a different point in the transaction lifecycle, and none of them work the same way.

01 · MEV-X Homelander

DEX MEV internalization For partners

Trades that would otherwise leak value to external searchers are captured at the point of execution and routed back internally, before they ever reach a public mempool.

swap intent→ internalized→ settled on-chain
Live on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism
02 · MEV-X Starlight

CEX–DEX arbitrage

Continuously prices venues against each other and closes spreads between centralized and decentralized order flow before they widen further.

CEX quote↔ spread engine↔ DEX quote
Live on Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain
03 · MEV-X Butcher

Liquidation engine

Monitors lending markets across chains and executes liquidations at the moment positions cross threshold, competing on latency, not luck.

position monitor→ threshold cross→ liquidation
Live on 6 lending markets, 5 chains
04 · MEV-X RPC

Partner order flow routing For partners

Send your transaction flow through a private MEV-X RPC endpoint and capture your own cut of the value it creates, with no rebate lost to a third-party searcher. Referral partners earn 25% of revenue from flow they bring in, two tiers deep.

partner flow→ private RPC→ value returned
